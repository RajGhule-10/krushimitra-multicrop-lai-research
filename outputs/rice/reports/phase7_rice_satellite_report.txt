7. SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS

1. The study area is represented by a temporary 100-metre buffer around a site-level coordinate.
2. Experimental plot boundaries are unavailable.
3. Pixel samples cannot be assumed to represent individual experimental rice plots.
4. Four acquisition dates provide limited temporal coverage.
5. Scene cloud percentage is scene-level metadata and does not represent cloud contamination for each sampled pixel.
6. Spectral indices are not direct measurements of leaf area index.

8. FINAL INTERPRETATION

The dataset supports exploratory analysis of site-level Sentinel-2 spectral behaviour and spatial variability across four acquisition dates.
The analysis does not establish plot-level rice monitoring accuracy or satellite-based LAI prediction.
