5. SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Satellite observations are sampled from a temporary 100 m site buffer.
- Experimental plot boundaries and plot-level coordinates are not available.
- A temporal date match does not establish spatial correspondence.
- Satellite vegetation indices are not direct LAI measurements.
- Four satellite acquisition dates do not provide continuous crop-season coverage.
- Ground-truth records have different experimental structures and treatment groupings.
- The nearest-date matching procedure does not establish a causal or predictive relationship.
- The suspicious Ranchi LAI observation remains excluded from model candidates according to Phase 4 processing.
- No satellite-to-LAI supervised model was trained.

6. FINAL CONCLUSION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The available Ranchi data support exploratory temporal assessment of site-level Sentinel-2 indices and ground-truth observation dates.

Temporal matching alone is insufficient to justify plot-level satellite-LAI integration or supervised LAI prediction. Georeferenced experimental plot boundaries, compatible field measurements, and independent validation are required.
